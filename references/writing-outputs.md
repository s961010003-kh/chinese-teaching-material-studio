# Writing outputs

All writing outputs must follow the same teaching unit model as the deck. Do not merely describe slide appearance.

## Teacher slide-by-slide script

For each slide provide:

- slide purpose;
- suggested spoken explanation in natural Traditional Chinese;
- observation or retrieval question;
- expected student response;
- likely misconception;
- teacher clarification with evidence;
- transition to the next slide;
- optional timing or differentiation note.

Write words the teacher can actually say. Explain technical terms immediately. Do not over-script routine classroom management.

## Standalone concept explainer

Recommended structure:

1. why the concept is difficult or useful;
2. central idea in learner-friendly language;
3. worked example;
4. visual/structural explanation;
5. confusing contrast or counterexample;
6. reusable decision process;
7. guided practice;
8. concise summary.

The article must remain understandable without the deck.

## Blog-ready teaching design/practice article

Recommended structure:

1. classroom or curriculum problem;
2. what the source material contains;
3. redesign goals and decisions;
4. how Scene and Framework support understanding;
5. walkthrough of representative teaching moves;
6. assessment design;
7. implementation status;
8. actual observations and adjustments, only when supplied;
9. reuse suggestions and artifact links.

Use first-person only when it matches the user's voice or supplied experience. Clearly label anticipated outcomes as predictions. Provide placeholders such as `[課後補記：學生反應]` when real implementation evidence is not yet available.

Avoid copying long source passages. Quote only what is needed for analysis and otherwise paraphrase.
